Import from Google My Maps or a spreadsheet

Accepts a CSV or a KML file. Columns are matched by header name, so the current My Maps columns (Position, Name (First and Last), Internship or Full-time?, Company, City, State, Graduation Year, Year of Opportunity) work as-is. Optional columns: Email, LinkedIn, Lat, Lon.

How to export the existing map
  1. Open the map in Google My Maps while signed in as an editor.
  2. Click the three dots next to the map title and choose Export to KML/KMZ. Pick the alumni layer, tick Export as KML instead of KMZ, and download.
  3. Drop that .kml file below. Pins keep their exact coordinates, so no geocoding is needed.
  4. Alternatively, open the layer's data table, copy everything into a Google Sheet, download it as CSV, and drop that here instead (cities get geocoded, about one per second).
